About

I like the part of risk work where you have to look at the evidence, understand what really matters and help the business make a decision. That can mean reviewing a SOC report, following up on a control gap, discussing a security clause in a contract or explaining the remaining risk to leadership.

Most of that work has been vendor due diligence, risk assessments, risk tiering, SOC 1 and SOC 2 reviews, remediation and continuous monitoring — at LSEG and MetLife, alongside Legal, Procurement, Information Security and business teams, and now at Compass Group across vendor onboarding, data access and technology integrations.

I moved to the United States in January 2025 for graduate study and completed an MS in Cybersecurity at UNC Charlotte in May 2026, with a 4.0 GPA and a focus on cyber risk, governance and policy. Before that I did an MBA, and my background is in business and finance.

I’m looking for TPRM, Vendor Risk, Cyber Risk or GRC roles where the work goes beyond completing a checklist and helps the business make better risk decisions.
